💥 POWERS & WEAPONS
Gravity Fist (Passive)

Go creates a localized gravitational force field ahead of his fist whenever he swings a punch.

  • Slower Punch = Bigger Boom: The slower and wider he swings, the more time his gravitational field has to continuously pressurize and crush surrounding air molecules together.
  • Explosive Release: The second his fist hits a target, all that trapped, super-pressurized air collapses instantly and releases externally in a massive explosion.
  • Heavy Resistance: Pressurizing all that air creates heavy resistance, making his fist feel super heavy to swing.
Combat Techniques
  • Quick Jab: Very fast punch, so there's no time to build up heavy air pressure. Delivers a quick, sharp, stingy pain to the enemy.
  • Big Swing: A big, slow winding swing that heavily pressurizes a massive pocket of air. Hits like a truck and causes a massive explosion.
  • Hydrogen Fist (Theoretical): Never done before, but if he swings slow enough with enough power, his gravity field could pressurize the air hard enough to trigger a mini nuclear explosion.
⚔️ STRENGTHS
  • Brawler / Infighter: Loves getting right in the enemy's face and beating them up close.
  • Tanky: Takes hits well so he can walk right up to enemies and land his punches.
  • Solid body: Has solid core and lower body strength that let him dodge easily and support his heavy punches.
🛡️ WEAKNESSES
  • No Ranged Attacks: Can't do anything against enemies who stay far away and shoot from distance.
  • Blast Recoil: The explosions affect him too. He has to plant his feet hard so he doesn't get blown back, which is stamina consuming.
  • Tunnel Vision: When he gets in close, he only focuses on what's right in front of him and loses sight of his sides.
👤 ORIGIN & LORE

Origin: Go's raw love for brawling is so intense that his stance and focus trigger a strange bio-electric reaction in his nervous system. When he winds up a punch with pure intent, his body creates a natural gravity field ahead of his fist—pressurizing air purely through his raw willpower and natural combat instinct.

Personality: A simple, straightforward guy. He doesn't care about the science or why his hands bend physics. If it lets him punch harder, he's happy with it!
